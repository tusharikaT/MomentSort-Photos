Interview Questions
Part 1 — Warm Up (2 min)
Build comfort, understand their relationship with their library


How long have you been using  Google Photos?
When was the last time you went back to find an old photo — what was the occasion?


Part 2 — A Specific Recent Failure (10 min)
The most important part — get the full episode, not a summary


Tell me about the last time you searched for a photo and it was frustrating or took longer than it should have.
(Once they describe it) Walk me through exactly what you did — step by step. What did you type first?(search bar or ask photos assistant)- any feature usage.
What were you thinking when the results came back?
At what point did you feel like you were stuck?
What did you do next? And after that?
Did you eventually find it? How?
How long did the whole thing take?
How did you feel at the end of it — whether you found it or not?


Part 3 — The Memory They Carry (5 min)
Understand the gap between what they remember and what they can type


When you were looking for that photo — what did you actually remember about it? Close your eyes and describe it to me like I can't see it.
Was there anything about it you just couldn't put into words?
What would you have needed to remember to find it faster?
Part 4 — The Moment of Giving Up (5 min)
Understand the emotional cost and the workaround


Have you ever completely given up on finding a photo? What happened?
When you switch to scrolling manually — what is that like? Walk me through it.
Has a photo ever felt truly lost to you — like you will never find it?
Does that bother you? What does it feel like to lose a memory like that?


Part 5 — Mental Model (5 min)
Understand what they wish existed — without leading them to your solution


If you could change one thing about how Google Photos search works, what would it be?
Imagine search worked perfectly for you — what would that look like?
Have you ever tried anything else to find photos — other apps, asking someone, anything?
Part 6 — Observation (if possible, 5 min)
Watch them actually search — the most valuable part


Can you show me on your phone? Search for a photo right now — any photo from more than a year ago that you haven't looked at recently.
(Watch silently, note every action)
Talk me through what you are thinking as you do this.
